We take on complete residential homes, boutique hospitality spaces, retail studios, and specialized modular kitchens. Our focus is on comprehensive, full-scope design where we can craft both the architecture of the room and bespoke joinery.

Our process moves in four organic phases: Initial Discovery & Spatial Planning, Material Curation & 3D Visualization, Detailed Technical Drawings, and On-Site Execution with artisan supervision until key handover.

Timeline varies by scale. A single-room transformation or modular kitchen typically takes 6 to 10 weeks, while a full residential home design and build spans anywhere from 4 to 8 months.

Yes, absolutely. While our design studio is based in Jaipur, we regularly execute projects across New Delhi, Gurugram, Mumbai, Goa, and Bengaluru through our curated network of regional craftsmen and project leads.

We offer structured square-footage design fees for conceptual planning paired with transparent cost-plus procurement for site execution. We outline every single detail before ground work begins, ensuring zero surprises.
